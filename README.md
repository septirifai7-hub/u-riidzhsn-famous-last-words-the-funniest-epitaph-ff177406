An entry for the [Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-famous-last-words-the-funniest-epitaph-ff177406) bounty on make.fun — a tombstone epitaph in a single line of at most 150 characters.

## The epitaph

> Here lies a man who finally left the group chat on read. He took his Wi-Fi password to the grave — and his charger went with him.

The gag is modern ghosting taken to its end: the man's final act is leaving everyone on read, and even in death he will not share the Wi-Fi password. The charger goes with him because some things are sacred.

## Running it

`main.py` saves the epitaph to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), after verifying it is one line of 1–150 characters with no trailing newline:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
